GROWTH-QUALITY CAVEAT: the Stage 1a 3-year growth figures (131.95% sales,
109.78% profit) and the secondary-sourced FY26 "3x revenue jump" both
describe extremely rapid, likely low-base-effect growth for a company that
appears to have scaled from a small revenue base to roughly Rs 1,064 crore
within a few years. This kind of percentage growth is structurally easier
to sustain briefly from a small base than indefinitely at the same rate --
flagged here explicitly rather than extrapolated forward at face value.
