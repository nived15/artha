BUSINESS DESCRIPTION

Ganesh Green Bharat Limited is described as a diversified renewable-energy
and infrastructure company listed on the NSE. Its core business is solar
EPC (Engineering, Procurement, and Construction) plus in-house solar
photovoltaic (PV) module manufacturing:

- Solar EPC: design, procurement, and deployment of both ground-mounted and
  rooftop solar power systems for government and industrial clients.
- PV Module Manufacturing: an automated module factory with current
  capacity of 1.1 GW, with stated plans to expand to 2 GW by 2027.
- Government Scheme Execution: solar water pumping systems under the
  PM-KUSUM scheme, and solar street lighting.
- Battery/Storage: manufacture of lithium-ion cells, including cells used
  in electric vehicles and energy-storage applications.
- Other Infrastructure Segments: electrical contracting (transmission and
  distribution lines, substations, street lighting) and water-supply-scheme
  projects under the Jal Jeevan Mission.

The company is described as using an asset-light EPC approach: it focuses
on project management, engineering, and procurement, outsourcing much of
the labor-intensive execution work. Its stated competitive advantage is
expertise in government tenders and decentralized, rural solar execution
(e.g. solar water pumps), which is described as a differentiated niche
relative to larger, more urban-focused solar EPC players.

INDUSTRY STRUCTURE

The Indian solar EPC/module-manufacturing sector is described as benefiting
from sustained government policy tailwinds: India's push for domestic
solar-module manufacturing (import-substitution policy), continued
government focus on renewable-energy capacity addition, rural
electrification programs, and decentralized solar-pump schemes (PM-KUSUM).
Competitive dynamics: the sector includes large, well-capitalized EPC
players as well as smaller regional players; GGBL's stated niche is
decentralized/rural execution and government-tender expertise rather than
head-to-head competition with the largest utility-scale EPC contractors.

FY2026 SCALE AND ORDER BOOK

Per secondary-source reporting (Multibagg.ai's FY26 write-up and
stockanalysis.com): FY26 revenue reported at approximately Rs 1,064 crore,
described as a roughly 3x increase over FY25. PAT reported at approximately
Rs 75.18 crore, versus approximately Rs 30.22 crore in FY25. FY26 EBITDA
margin reported at approximately 10.67%, net margin at approximately 7.06%
-- both markedly lower than the Stage 1a snapshot's OPM figure of 10.43%
for the trailing-3-year-average period, which is broadly consistent
(single-digit operating margins are typical of EPC/contracting business
models, unlike asset-light services businesses). The company's total
secured order book is reported at approximately Rs 2,212 crore as of FY26,
described as nearly twice its annual revenue, providing forward revenue
visibility.

STRATEGIC INITIATIVES

Per secondary-source reporting, the company is pursuing diversification
into battery energy storage EPC and EV-charging infrastructure, alongside
larger-scale ground-mounted solar projects. It holds ISO 9001:2015 (Quality)
and ISO 14001:2015 (Environment) certifications, and is stated to have BIS
compliance for its manufactured products. Reported headcount is over 150
permanent employees, with significant reliance on contract staffing for
field execution -- a structural feature of asset-light EPC business models
that increases execution flexibility but also means a large share of
delivery capability is not directly controlled/owned.
