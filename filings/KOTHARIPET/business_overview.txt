BUSINESS DESCRIPTION

Kothari Petrochemicals Limited is primarily engaged in the manufacture and
sale of Polyisobutylene (PIB), a specialty petrochemical polymer, and
related petrochemical products. The company is described as the largest
producer of premium-quality PIB in India. PIB is used across a wide range
of industrial applications, including automotive lubricant additives,
adhesives, sealants, plastic masterbatches, and other technical/industrial
formulations. The company supplies to both domestic Indian customers and
export markets, with multiple grades of PIB manufactured for different
technical applications. (Correction/clarification versus an earlier
research assumption: this company's core business is Polyisobutylene, NOT
recycled polyester or PET recycling -- no evidence was found of a recycled
polyester business line.)

INDUSTRY STRUCTURE

PIB manufacturing is a specialty/technical petrochemical segment, distinct
from bulk commodity petrochemicals -- it is characterized by fewer
producers, technical product differentiation by grade/molecular weight,
and demand tied to automotive lubricants, industrial adhesives/sealants,
and plastics processing. As a petrochemical input manufacturer, the
business is exposed to crude-oil/feedstock price volatility and cyclical
industrial demand, though being the largest premium-PIB producer in India
is described as a scale/technical-differentiation position within this
niche rather than a commodity-scale cost position.

FINANCIAL PROFILE

The company is a long-established, dividend-paying petrochemical
manufacturer (part of the H C Kothari Group) rather than a recent listing.
