KOTHARI PETROCHEMICALS LIMITED (NSE: KOTHARIPET)
Shareholding and governance notes

Source: web research synthesizing Trendlyne's shareholding-pattern page,
the company's own investor-relations page (kotharipetrochemicals.com),
and NSE's archived annual-report filing. Captured via web research on
2026-08-30.

PROMOTER SHAREHOLDING: Promoter holding has remained steady at
approximately 72.2% across quarters from June 2025 through June 2026 (per
Trendlyne's shareholding-pattern tracker). Pledged shares are reported at
0.0% for all recent quarters -- no promoter shares are pledged, and no new
pledges or encumbrances were reported in FY2025. This is consistent with
the Stage 1a snapshot's own promoter_pledge_pct=0.0 and a modestly rising
(+1.24) 3-year holding trend.

GROUP AFFILIATION: Kothari Petrochemicals is described as part of the H C
Kothari Group, a long-established Indian industrial group -- distinct from
a first-generation promoter-founder company (like GGBL or Virtual Galaxy in
this same research batch), this is an established group-company structure
with a multi-decade operating and listing history.

RELATED PARTY TRANSACTIONS AND AUDITOR: the company's FY2024-25 Annual
Report (publicly available as a PDF via both the company's own investor-
relations page and NSE's archive, archives.nseindia.com) is stated to
contain a dedicated "Notes Forming Part of Financial Statements" section
covering related-party transactions, management remuneration, and board
details. This research pass located the existence and URL of this primary
document but did NOT independently read its related-party-transaction
tables or confirm the statutory auditor's name/tenure/opinion status line
by line -- these specific governance facts are reported as unverified
(location known, content not independently parsed) rather than assumed
clean.

SEBI / REGULATORY FLAGS: no SEBI show-cause order, adverse
related-party-transaction finding, or auditor resignation was found
referenced in the sources consulted for this research pass. As with the
other candidates in this batch, this is an absence of finding in general
public/financial-portal research, not an exhaustive review of SEBI's
enforcement database or the company's full multi-decade exchange-filing
history.

DIVIDEND-PAYING TRACK RECORD AS A GOVERNANCE SIGNAL: a company that
consistently pays out cash dividends (interim Rs 1.00/share Aug 2025,
final Rs 1.00/share Jul 2024, 5-year dividend growth ~28.47% per
investing.com) provides a weak but real corroborating signal of genuine,
cash-backed (not purely accrual) profitability, since dividends must be
funded from actual distributable cash reserves -- this is noted as a
partial, non-conclusive cross-check on earnings quality, not a substitute
for an independently verified OCF/PAT figure (which remains unavailable,
per the financial-highlights document).
