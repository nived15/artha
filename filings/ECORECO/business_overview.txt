BUSINESS DESCRIPTION

Eco Recycling Limited is described as a pioneer in India's e-waste
management sector, incorporated in 1994 (per public company-registry
records, CIN L74120MH1994PLC079971). Its business model is described as
vertically integrated across the e-waste value chain:

- Collection & Reverse Logistics: pan-India collection of end-of-life
  electronics.
- Data Destruction: secure destruction of sensitive storage devices.
- Refurbishment & IT Asset Disposition (ITAD): recovering and extending the
  life of electronics where possible.
- Material Recovery: extracting precious, rare, and base metals from
  e-waste for recycling.
- Remarketing: reselling refurbished devices and recovered components.
- EPR Compliance services: helping electronics manufacturers (OEMs) meet
  their Extended Producer Responsibility obligations under India's
  E-Waste Management Rules, 2022.
- Other services: lamp recycling, battery recycling, a mobile collection
  service branded "Recycling on Wheels — SmartER", and corporate/school
  collection drives.

The company's recycling facility is described as CPCB (Central Pollution
Control Board) and MPCB (Maharashtra Pollution Control Board) authorized,
and R2v3 ("Responsible Recycling") certified, with a stated processing
capacity of up to 31,200 metric tonnes per annum.

INDUSTRY STRUCTURE AND NAMED COMPETITORS

The formal (licensed/certified) Indian e-waste recycling sector is
described as including, alongside Ecoreco: EMS Ltd (described as the
market leader), Antony Waste Handling Cell, Concord Enviro Systems,
Hi-Green Carbon Ltd, Race Eco Chain Ltd, and Organic Recycling Systems Ltd.
Competitive dynamics described: growth driven by rising electronics
consumption, digitization, and tightening enforcement of the E-Waste
Management Rules 2022; challenges include competition from the informal
(unlicensed) recycling sector, short electronics product life cycles, and
the need for continuous technology adaptation.

Relative to the named peers, Ecoreco is described as: nearly debt-free,
among the highest operating margins in the peer set, and having shown
strong profit growth over the trailing five years (a reported CAGR range
of 44-99%, per the secondary-source summary — treated here as a rough
directional signal rather than an exact, independently confirmed figure).

STRATEGIC INITIATIVES

The company's FY2025 Management Discussion & Analysis (as summarized by
secondary sources) describes: continued technology investment, new
collection models (e.g. "Recycling on Wheels"), and compliance leadership
as strategic priorities, and states the company has considered a strategic
partnership with ERI (a US-based recycler) to deepen technology and
best-practices adoption. It frames the company's positioning as aiming to
be OEMs' and corporates' preferred formal-sector partner for e-waste
compliance, data-security, and sustainability needs.
