MODI NATURALS LIMITED (NSE: MODINATUR)
Shareholding and governance notes

Source: web research synthesizing Trendlyne's shareholding-pattern page,
Tickjournal's shareholding tracker, and indianstockalerts.com's related-
party-transaction summary page. Captured via web research on 2026-08-30.

PROMOTER SHAREHOLDING: Promoter holding remained steady at approximately
69.09% throughout FY2025 and into 2026, consistent for several preceding
quarters (Trendlyne). No promoter shares were pledged during this period
(0.00% pledge, consistent with the Stage 1a snapshot). Institutional
participation (FII/DII) is reported as negligible/not notable; public
shareholding is approximately 30.9%.

RELATED PARTY TRANSACTIONS: Modi Naturals maintains a published "Policy on
Dealing with Related Party Transactions" on its investor-relations page.
Per indianstockalerts.com's related-party-transaction summary page and
general public disclosures, there is no reported material or unusual
related-party transaction for FY2025 -- routine transactions typical of a
family-promoter-controlled company exist, but nothing flagged as an
exception in the sources consulted. This research pass did NOT
independently read the primary annual report's related-party-transaction
notes table line by line, so the absence-of-finding above should be read
as "not flagged by secondary sources," not as an independently verified
clean bill.

AUDITOR AND SEBI FLAGS: this research pass did not locate the statutory
auditor's name, tenure, or audit-opinion status, nor any SEBI show-cause
order, adverse RPT finding, or auditor resignation referenced in the
sources consulted. As with the other candidates in this batch, this is an
absence of finding in general public research, not an exhaustive
regulatory-database review.

FAMILY-PROMOTER CONTROL STRUCTURE: the company is described as a
family-run business (the "Modi" family name is the company's own name),
similar in structure to GGBL (Patel family) in this same research batch --
a single-family promoter control structure with a high, stable, unpledged
holding is a genuinely positive integrity signal on the specific dimensions
this pipeline can check (pledge, holding trend), but it also means board
independence and professional-management depth cannot be assumed without
further verification.
