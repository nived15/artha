GROWTH-QUALITY CAVEAT (the specific concern flagged in this research pass
and cross-referenced in the Ecoreco dossier considered in the same
session): the Stage 1a 3-year profit growth figure (248.36%) is very high
relative to the much more moderate 3-year sales growth figure (19.85%) --
this combination (profit growing far faster than sales) is a classic
low-base-effect or margin-expansion-from-a-depressed-prior-year pattern,
and should NOT be read as "profits compounding at 248%/year going
forward." The company's own quarterly revenue trend (financial highlights,
business overview document) shows a genuinely mixed, not smoothly
compounding, sequential pattern (Rs 189.9cr to Rs 155.0cr to Rs 147.0cr to
Rs 174.1cr to Rs 243.1cr across five quarters) -- this is the concrete,
cited evidence behind the caution, not a generic disclaimer.
