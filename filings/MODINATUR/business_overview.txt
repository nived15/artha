BUSINESS DESCRIPTION

Modi Naturals Limited is an Indian branded consumer-foods and edible-oils
company. Its legacy core business is the processing and marketing of rice
bran oil, and it is described as one of the largest processors/marketers of
rice bran oil in India, and as the 3rd-largest premium edible-oil brand in
the country. Its flagship consumer brand is "Oleev" (olive oil, olive/rice
bran blends, health oil, "smart" oil variants), alongside "Rizolo" (rice
bran oil), "Miller" (canola oil), and "Olivana" (wellness oil). The
business operates through two segments: a branded/retail consumer-products
business and a bulk (B2B) edible-oils business.

DIVERSIFICATION -- ETHANOL: the company has announced diversification into

INDUSTRY STRUCTURE

The Indian edible-oils sector is a large, competitive, brand-differentiated
consumer category with established national players (e.g. Adani Wilmar,
Marico, Ruchi Soya/Patanjali Foods) alongside smaller branded and
unbranded/commodity players. Modi Naturals' stated positioning is a
premium, health-oriented niche (rice bran oil, olive oil blends) rather
than head-to-head competition on the largest mass-market edible-oil
categories (e.g. mustard, soyabean, palm oil). Edible oils as a category
are exposed to volatile global commodity input costs (crude palm oil,
soyabean, sunflower oil import prices) which flow through to margins with
a lag; this is a structural industry-level risk distinct from any
company-specific execution issue.
