BUSINESS DESCRIPTION

Virtual Galaxy Infotech Limited (VGIL) is an Indian IT and enterprise
software company headquartered in Nagpur, Maharashtra, founded in 1997 --
i.e. it has approximately three decades of pre-listing operating history,
despite being a comparatively recent stock-market listing. It specializes
in SaaS-based solutions across banking, enterprise resource planning
(ERP), e-governance, and custom IT systems:

- Core Banking: its flagship product, "E-Banker," is a core-banking
  solution reportedly deployed across over 5,000 bank branches.
- ERP: enterprise resource planning systems for business automation.
- E-governance platforms: named products include "E-APMC" (agricultural
  market/mandi automation) and "E-Autopsy" (forensic-department
  digitization), among others.
- Tax/compliance and payments: a tax-compliance product ("VGST") and a
  digital-payments product ("V-Pay").
- Cloud/infrastructure services: IaaS, SaaS, hosting, storage, backup, and
  migration services, plus cybersecurity and conversational-AI offerings.

Industry verticals served: BFSI (Banking, Financial Services, Insurance),
government corporations/agencies, and industrial enterprises.

LISTING STATUS -- IMPORTANT RISK FLAG

VGIL is listed on the NSE SME (NSE Emerge) platform, NOT the NSE/BSE
mainboard. Its IPO raised approximately Rs 93.3 crore. SME-platform
listings in India carry materially different risk characteristics from
mainboard listings: lower minimum public float and lot-size-based trading
(reducing retail liquidity), lighter continuous-disclosure obligations
than mainboard-listed companies, thinner analyst/institutional coverage,
and a much shorter public listed-market history over which price/volume
patterns, promoter conduct, and disclosure quality can be independently
observed. This is flagged explicitly and prominently, not treated as
incidental -- it is the single most material structural risk factor for
this specific candidate, distinct from (and additive to) its business
fundamentals.

INDUSTRY STRUCTURE

VGIL competes in India's core-banking-software and e-governance IT-services
market, a segment with several established larger listed/unlisted players
(e.g. Infosys Finacle, TCS BaNCS, FIS, Oracle FLEXCUBE at the large-bank
end; a longer tail of smaller regional core-banking vendors serving
cooperative/regional/rural banks at the smaller end, which appears to be
VGIL's actual competitive tier given its scale). No specific named client
list or competitor comparison was found in the sources consulted for this
research pass -- VGIL's precise competitive positioning within the
smaller-bank/cooperative-bank segment is asserted by the company's own
marketing material but not independently corroborated here.
