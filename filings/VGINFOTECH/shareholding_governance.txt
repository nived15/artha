VIRTUAL GALAXY INFOTECH LIMITED (NSE SME/Emerge: VGINFOTECH)
Shareholding and governance notes

Source: web research synthesizing Trendlyne's shareholding-pattern page and
Upstox's shareholding-pattern page. Captured via web research on
2026-08-30.

PROMOTER SHAREHOLDING: Promoter holding is reported steady at
approximately 65.00% for all quarters through FY2025-26 (Trendlyne),
consistent with the Stage 1a snapshot's promoter_holding_pct=65.0. No
promoter shares are pledged (0% pledged) for all recent quarters. Notably,
over 99.5% of promoter shares are reported as locked-in for most quarters
-- a standard SEBI SME-IPO requirement (a longer mandatory lock-in period
than mainboard IPOs), which is a structural, regulation-driven feature of
the SME listing rather than a discretionary promoter commitment, and
should not be read as a voluntary signal of extra promoter conviction.

INSTITUTIONAL HOLDING: Foreign Institutional Investor (FII) holding has
DECLINED from approximately 5.0% in earlier quarters to approximately
1.12% as of June 2026. Domestic Institutional Investor (DII) holding is
approximately 1.18%. This declining FII trend, while starting from an
already-small base, is flagged explicitly as a data point rather than
omitted -- it could reflect routine small-holder portfolio churn typical
of thinly-held SME stocks, or could reflect an institutional investor
reducing conviction; this research pass could not distinguish between
those explanations.

RELATED PARTY TRANSACTIONS, AUDITOR, AND SEBI FLAGS: no related-party-
transaction quantum, auditor name/tenure/opinion, or SEBI show-cause/
adverse-finding disclosure was located in the sources consulted for this
research pass. Given the SME-platform listing's lighter disclosure regime
(noted in the business-overview document), the absence of a public,
easily-searchable secondary-source discussion of these items is a weaker
signal here than it would be for a longer-listed mainboard company like
Kothari Petrochemicals -- i.e. "not found" should be weighted as "not yet
verifiable from public secondary sources," not as "confirmed clean."

STRUCTURAL RISK SUMMARY: this is the highest-risk candidate of the four in
this research batch on governance/disclosure-quality grounds specifically
(not on reported-fundamentals grounds) -- a genuinely small, SME-platform,
thinly-institutionally-held, single-promoter-family company with a
three-decade pre-listing operating history but a very short (SME-listing)
public-market disclosure history. This should be weighted accordingly in
the Integrity Gate and Fatal-Flaw Checklist sections rather than treated
as equivalent in verifiability to a mainboard-listed peer.
