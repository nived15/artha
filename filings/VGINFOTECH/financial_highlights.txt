GROWTH-QUALITY CAVEAT: as with several other candidates in this research
batch, the very high percentage growth figures (194.74% 3-year profit
growth per Stage 1a; ~190% YoY quarterly revenue growth per the Q4
FY2025-26 figure) are measured off what is still a genuinely small
absolute revenue base (~Rs 120 crore FY2025 revenue; ~Rs 19-55 crore
per-quarter revenue). This is the same low-base-effect caution flagged for
GGBL and Modi Naturals in this batch -- extremely high percentage growth
off a small base is structurally easier to sustain briefly and harder to
sustain indefinitely, and should not be extrapolated forward at face
value without independent confirmation of a multi-year, not single-quarter,
trend.
