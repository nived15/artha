## 2. The business in five sentences

Modi Naturals Limited is an Indian branded consumer-foods and edible-oils company, one of the largest processors/marketers of rice bran oil in India and described as the 3rd-largest premium edible-oil brand in the country. Its flagship brand is 'Oleev' (olive oil and olive/rice-bran blends), alongside 'Rizolo' (rice bran oil), 'Miller' (canola oil), and 'Olivana' (wellness oil), sold through a branded/retail segment and a bulk (B2B) edible-oils segment. It has announced diversification into grain-based ethanol/distillery operations via subsidiary Modi Biotech Pvt Ltd, a stated future growth vertical whose financial contribution is not yet separately disclosed. It is a mainboard-listed (NSE: MODINATUR, BSE: 519003), family-promoter-controlled company (the Modi family), holding 69.09%, zero pledge, rising trend. Its quarterly revenue trend has been genuinely mixed (declining sequentially for three quarters before a sharp Mar'26 spike), not a smoothly compounding growth story.
