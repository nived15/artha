## 2. Business in Five Sentences

Eco Recycling Limited collects end-of-life electronics across India through reverse logistics, collection drives, and related channels. It provides secure data destruction and tries to refurbish, remarket, or extend the life of usable devices and components. For residual e-waste, it recovers precious, rare, and base metals through recycling operations. It also sells EPR compliance services to OEMs and corporates that need formal disposal under the E-Waste Management Rules, 2022. The present business claim rests on being a licensed, certified formal-sector operator with CPCB/MPCB authorizations, R2v3 certification, and stated processing capacity of up to 31,200 metric tonnes per annum.
